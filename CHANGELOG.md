# Changelog

## 0.3.2 (2026-10-18)

0.3.2 moves mine onto liaise 0.2.4 and abut 0.2.2. 0.3.1 still asked for liaise ^0.1.6, so anything that used mine next to liaise 0.2 built liaise twice, and mine's codes implemented the old `Liaise` trait. abut 0.2.2 is the same move on abut's side. nothing else changed. every code and message renders byte for byte the same as 0.3.1.

### Cargo.toml

- liaise was 0.1.6: [Cargo.toml L24-L24](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/efdaa8ba98fb7bce92a49a2139d624b971981e23/Cargo.toml#L24-L24). it's 0.2.4 now, still with `std`: [Cargo.toml L24-L24](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/Cargo.toml#L24-L24). the derive under it goes from liaise_derive 0.2.3 to 0.3.4.
- abut was ^0.2.0, which let in abut 0.2.1 and its liaise 0.1.6: [Cargo.toml L23-L23](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/efdaa8ba98fb7bce92a49a2139d624b971981e23/Cargo.toml#L23-L23). it's ^0.2.2 now, the abut on liaise 0.2.4: [Cargo.toml L23-L23](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/Cargo.toml#L23-L23).
- version 0.3.1 to 0.3.2: [Cargo.toml L3-L3](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/efdaa8ba98fb7bce92a49a2139d624b971981e23/Cargo.toml#L3-L3), [Cargo.toml L3-L3](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/Cargo.toml#L3-L3).

### the derive

- liaise_derive 0.2.3 wrote `Display` as `self.render()`, so the `Liaise` trait had to be in scope wherever `#[derive(LiaiseCodes)]` was, and error.rs and classified_frames.rs imported it for that alone: [src/error.rs L12-L12](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/efdaa8ba98fb7bce92a49a2139d624b971981e23/src/error.rs#L12-L12), [src/classified_frames.rs L13-L13](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/efdaa8ba98fb7bce92a49a2139d624b971981e23/src/classified_frames.rs#L13-L13). 0.3.4 writes `::liaise::Liaise::render(self)`, so both imports went unused and are just `LiaiseCodes` now: [src/error.rs L12-L12](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/src/error.rs#L12-L12), [src/classified_frames.rs L13-L13](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/src/classified_frames.rs#L13-L13).
- `source` on `RecvError` works the same in 0.3.4: `Error::source()` is the frame or container error and there's a `From` for each, so `?` still lifts them: [src/classified_frames.rs L19-L26](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/src/classified_frames.rs#L19-L26).
- the derive also gives `MineCode` and `RecvError` the rest of liaise 0.2's `Liaise` trait (`code`, `slug`, `severity`, `stability`, `descriptor`, `liaise_report` and the others), all defaults since mine sets none of them. `prefix` is a method in 0.2, `code.prefix()`, where it was `MineCode::prefix()` in 0.1. mine never calls it. `pub use liaise;` is liaise 0.2.4 now: [src/lib.rs L68-L68](https://github.com/jameshuntt/mine/blob/0f522f8218e3b6d1271c258539656e26064c978d/src/lib.rs#L68-L68). no mine item was added or removed: the `cargo doc` item list is the same 32 items before and after.

### proof

- parity: one program, built once against the published abut 0.2.1 and mine 0.3.1 (liaise 0.1.6) and once against abut 0.2.2 and mine 0.3.2 (liaise 0.2.4), prints `PREFIX`, `ALL_CODES`, `code_id`, `message`, `render` and `Display` for every `MineCode` and `RecvError` variant, `Display` and `source` for each one, the `io::Error` carrying each `MineCode`, and what `code_of` gets back out of it. the two outputs are the same 94 lines, sha256 33f2d9eea9226fc5cd7617b0f171b0743d385fd0c01b16b47b128add47782ca8.
- a crate using mine with `classified` and `peercred` next to liaise 0.2.4 builds with one liaise in its tree, sends a classified frame through `accept_from(&Admit::me())` and back, and calls liaise 0.2.4's `code()` and `render()` straight on `AbutCode`, `MineCode` and `RecvError`.
